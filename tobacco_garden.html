Our Story

Not a Chain.
A Community Shop.

Tobacco Garden isn't a franchise and it isn't a corporate outpost. It's a Black-owned, woman-run shop on Hwy 28 in Anderson — built to serve the community with good products, fair prices, and the kind of service you don't get when a company is running thousands of locations at once.

When you spend money here, it stays local. That matters to a lot of people. It matters to us too.
