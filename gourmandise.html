Our Story

A Taste of Paris, Right Here in Anderson

⚜

Gourmandise brings the tradition of French artisan baking to the heart of South Carolina. Every croissant, éclair, and macaron is made from scratch using classic techniques passed down through generations of French pastry craft. Whether you're stopping in for your morning café au lait or ordering a custom cake for a special occasion, we bring a little bit of Paris to your day.

"Good bread is the most fundamentally satisfying of all foods — and good pastry is the bread of celebration."
